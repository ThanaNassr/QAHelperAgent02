## Summary Review Criteria

# 1.Accuracy 
Names, amounts, dates and time, account details and products should match call transcirpt.


# 2.Unsupported Claims 
Everything in the summary should be traced back to something in the transcript 

# 3.Missing information 
Nothing imporrtant should be missing, including complaints, promises, escalations, deadlines, and follow ups..

# 4. Call Outcomes and next steps
Explicit mention of call outcome and whether resolution was reached or not and what happens next.

# 5. Attribution 
Summary should accurately reflect who said what (customer / agent)

# 6. Severity of Issues 
This applies to all criteria above 
Severity Scale:
0 to 5: 
0 (None) : This criteria does not have any issues
1 (Trivial) : Spelling or grammatical errors, rephrased sentences but meaning is the same
2 (Minor) : irrelevant details missing or slighlty imprecise informaiton
3 (Moderate) : nothing wrong in the summary but leaves information that the next person would need to take correct action. If acted on the summary alone , they could act incorrectly or incompletely. 
4 (Major): Wrong or missing information likely to lead to wrong action 
5 (Critical) : directly wrong facts or outcome that could cause real harm if acted upon.
